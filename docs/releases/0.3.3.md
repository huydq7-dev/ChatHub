# ChatHub 0.3.3 — notification controls, privacy, and multi-account workflow

Integrates the feature PRs #2–#6 on top of 0.3.2.

## Notifications

- Per-account mute and snooze (1 hour or until 8 AM), plus daily quiet hours.
  Muted accounts are excluded from the Dock and menu bar counts.
- No alert for a conversation that is on screen while ChatHub is active.
- A title count in the page is now the authoritative unread count, so services without
  a DOM selector no longer flip between the count and zero.
- Optional setting to hide message previews in notifications.
- Signed-out warning: when a service that had been signed in asks to sign in again, the
  account shows an indicator and raises one notification. Zalo, Messenger and Discord are
  detected from the page address; Telegram from its sign-in screen.

## Background accounts

- Accounts load one at a time after launch (Settings → Notifications to turn off).
- Muted accounts that sit idle for 1, 4 or 12 hours release their web session and reopen
  when selected or unmuted. Accounts with notifications and with active downloads never sleep.

## Workspace

- Menu bar item and Dock menu list accounts with unread messages first and hide names
  while the app is locked. Next Unread Account (⌘J).
- Optional app lock with Touch ID or the login password (Immediately, 1, 5 or 15 minutes;
  also on sleep, screen lock and user switch).
- Drag accounts in the sidebar to reorder them; pin accounts to the top.
- Split view: open a second account beside the selected one.
- Downloads list in the sidebar footer; per-account browser for opening links; open at login.

## Integration notes

- The release branch removed the window toolbar, so the downloads button lives in the
  sidebar footer and split-view controls live in the pane headers and the Workspace menu.
  `WorkspaceToolbar` is unchanged and unused, as in 0.3.2.
- The release menu bar (with Privacy Shield and Always on Top) is kept; the unread-first
  list and the lock behaviour were added to it. There is a single menu bar item.
- Notification delivery stays in `HubNotificationCenter` inside `BrowserSession.swift`
  (the Node bridge tests read it from there); mute, snooze, quiet hours and preview hiding
  are applied at its single `postNotification` entry point.
